Source: Central Board of Indirect Taxes — GST Guidance for MSMEs
Applicable State: central
Applicable Industry: All
Regulation Category: Taxation
Last Verified Date: 2026-04-20
Effective Date: 2023-01-01

Section 1: GST Registration Thresholds
1.1 Businesses supplying goods with an aggregate annual turnover exceeding INR 40 lakh must register for GST. This threshold is INR 20 lakh for special category states.
1.2 Businesses supplying services with an aggregate annual turnover exceeding INR 20 lakh must register for GST. This threshold is INR 10 lakh for special category states.
1.3 Businesses engaged exclusively in intra-state supply and opting for the Composition Scheme may register with turnover up to INR 1.5 crore, subject to eligibility conditions.

Section 2: Composition Scheme for MSMEs
2.1 Eligible manufacturers and traders may opt for the Composition Scheme, paying tax at a concessional rate of 1% of turnover, in lieu of standard GST rates.
2.2 Restaurant service providers (not serving alcohol) may opt for the Composition Scheme at a concessional rate of 5% of turnover.
2.3 Businesses under the Composition Scheme cannot collect GST from customers or claim input tax credit.

Section 3: Filing Requirements
3.1 Regular GST-registered businesses must file monthly returns (GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B) by the prescribed due dates.
3.2 Businesses under the Composition Scheme must file quarterly statements and an annual return (GSTR-4).
3.3 Businesses with turnover up to INR 5 crore may opt for the QRMP (Quarterly Return, Monthly Payment) scheme.

Section 4: Penalties for Non-Compliance
4.1 Late filing of GST returns attracts a late fee per day of delay, subject to a maximum cap, in addition to interest on any unpaid tax liability.
4.2 Failure to register despite exceeding the applicable threshold may attract a penalty of 10% of the tax due, subject to a minimum amount, or the tax amount itself in cases of deliberate evasion.

Section 5: MSME-Specific Relief
5.1 Micro and small enterprises registered under Udyam Registration may be eligible for delayed payment protection under the MSME Development Act, which operates independently of GST compliance obligations.
5.2 This section does not exempt any business from GST registration or filing obligations regardless of Udyam registration status.
