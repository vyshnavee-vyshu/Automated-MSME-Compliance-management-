Source: Tamil Nadu Shops and Establishments Act — MSME Labor Compliance Guidance
Applicable State: Tamil Nadu
Applicable Industry: All
Regulation Category: Labor
Last Verified Date: 2026-05-15
Effective Date: 2023-04-01

Section 1: Registration
1.1 Every shop or commercial establishment must register with the local labor department within 30 days of commencing business.
1.2 Registration must be renewed every 5 years, or immediately upon any change in the nature of business, ownership, or address.
1.3 Establishments employing fewer than 5 workers may apply for simplified registration under the MSME single-window scheme.

Section 2: Working Hours
2.1 No employee shall be required to work more than 8 hours in a day or 48 hours in a week.
2.2 Overtime work is permitted up to 12 hours per week, subject to payment of overtime wages at twice the ordinary rate.
2.3 Every employee is entitled to a weekly holiday of at least 24 consecutive hours.

Section 3: Employee Welfare and Leave
3.1 Employees are entitled to a minimum of 12 days of paid earned leave per year, accruing after completion of 240 days of continuous service.
3.2 Establishments employing more than 10 workers must provide a first-aid box and maintain a register of leave and attendance.
3.3 Female employees are entitled to maternity benefits under applicable central legislation, in addition to state leave provisions.

Section 4: Wage Payment
4.1 Wages must be paid on or before the 7th day of the following month for establishments with fewer than 1000 employees.
4.2 Wage payment must be made through bank transfer for establishments with more than 20 employees, per digital wage payment guidance.

Section 5: Applicability Thresholds
5.1 This Act applies to all shops, commercial establishments, and factories operating within Tamil Nadu, excluding purely agricultural operations.
5.2 Establishments employing fewer than 5 workers are exempt from Sections 2.2 and 3.2 but remain subject to all other provisions.

Section 6: Penalties
6.1 Non-compliance with registration requirements may attract a fine determined by the labor commissioner's office, in addition to potential closure notice for repeat violations.
