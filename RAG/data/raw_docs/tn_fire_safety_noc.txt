Source: Tamil Nadu Fire and Rescue Services — Fire Safety No-Objection Certificate (NOC) Guidance for MSMEs
Applicable State: Tamil Nadu
Applicable Industry: All
Regulation Category: Environmental
Last Verified Date: 2026-06-01
Effective Date: 2024-01-01

Section 1: Applicability
1.1 Every commercial, industrial, or manufacturing establishment operating within Tamil Nadu with a built-up area exceeding 500 square meters, or employing more than 20 workers, must obtain a Fire Safety No-Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Tamil Nadu Fire and Rescue Services before commencing operations.
1.2 Establishments handling hazardous or flammable materials, regardless of size, must obtain a Fire Safety NOC.
1.3 Micro enterprises with built-up area below 500 square meters and fewer than 10 workers, and not handling hazardous materials, are exempt from mandatory NOC but are encouraged to obtain a voluntary fire safety inspection certificate.

Section 2: Application Process
2.1 Applications must be submitted through the Tamil Nadu Single Window Portal along with the building plan, occupancy certificate, and details of fire-fighting equipment installed.
2.2 A physical inspection by a Fire Station Officer is mandatory before NOC issuance.
2.3 The standard processing time is 30 working days from the date of complete application submission.

Section 3: Renewal Requirements
3.1 Fire Safety NOCs are valid for a period of 3 years from the date of issuance.
3.2 Renewal applications must be submitted at least 60 days before expiry.
3.3 Any structural modification to the premises requires a fresh NOC application, irrespective of the remaining validity of the existing certificate.

Section 4: Penalties for Non-Compliance
4.1 Operating without a valid Fire Safety NOC where one is required may result in a penalty and, in cases of continued non-compliance, closure orders under the Tamil Nadu Fire Service Act.
4.2 Penalty amounts vary by establishment size and are determined by the jurisdictional Fire Station Officer at the time of inspection.

Section 5: Required Fire Safety Equipment (Minimum Standards)
5.1 Portable fire extinguishers appropriate to the fire risk class of the premises.
5.2 Clearly marked emergency exits with illuminated signage.
5.3 Fire alarm systems for establishments exceeding 1000 square meters built-up area.
5.4 Establishments in this category must also maintain a fire safety log book, inspected periodically by the Fire and Rescue Services.
